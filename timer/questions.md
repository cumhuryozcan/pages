# Support Page Questions

Please answer these questions before the support page is generated:

1. Is the app name exactly **Timer One**, and should the support page use any tagline or developer/company name?
   **Answer:** Timer One only.
2. What is the simplest one-sentence description of the app that you want visitors to see first?
   **Answer:** Use "Set, start, that's it" as the main description, with "Set a duration, start the countdown, and hear a tone when time is up." as the supporting subtitle.
3. Which Apple platforms and device types does the app support, and what is the minimum iOS or iPadOS version?
   **Answer:** iPhone, iOS 15+.
4. How does a user set a timer duration: a picker, preset buttons, manual input, or another control?
   **Answer:** Both a duration picker and preset buttons.
5. What duration range and precision are supported, such as seconds, minutes, hours, or a maximum duration?
   **Answer:** Durations can use seconds, minutes, and hours. The maximum duration is 99 hours, 59 minutes, and 59 seconds.
6. Which actions are available while a timer is running, such as pause, resume, restart, cancel, or editing the duration?
   **Answer:** Pause, resume, and reset.
7. Can users save, reuse, or manage presets or multiple timers, or is there only one active timer?
   **Answer:** One active timer with built-in presets.
8. What exactly happens when the countdown reaches zero: sound, vibration, notification, visual change, or a combination?
   **Answer:** The app plays a sound and sends a notification.
9. Can users choose or disable the completion sound, and does the timer respect Silent Mode, Focus, and volume settings?
   **Answer:** Sound is fixed and cannot be silenced.
10. Does the timer continue running when the app is in the background, the device is locked, or the user switches to another app?
   **Answer:** The timer continues running in the background, on the Lock Screen, and after switching apps.
11. What should users expect if the app is force-quit, the device restarts, or an incoming call or other interruption occurs during a timer?
   **Answer:** Skipped.
12. Does the app request any permissions, such as notifications, and what should a user do if a permission is denied?
   **Answer:** Notifications are optional. If notification access is denied, the timer still runs but no notification is delivered.
13. What are the most likely problems users may need help with, and what troubleshooting steps should the page recommend?
   **Answer:** Skipped.
14. Does the app require an internet connection, an account, or any external service to start and use a timer?
   **Answer:** The app works fully offline, without an account or external service.
15. Which languages and accessibility features are supported, such as Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, or increased contrast?
   **Answer:** Multiple languages.
16. What support email address or contact URL should users use to report a problem or ask a question?
   **Answer:** There is no support email address or external contact URL; this HTML page will be the support destination.
17. What information should users include in a support request, such as device model, OS version, app version, and reproduction steps?
   **Answer:** Skipped.
18. Does the app have paid features, subscriptions, or purchases that require billing, restore-purchase, or refund guidance?
   **Answer:** The app is free and has no purchases.
19. Which links should the page include, such as the privacy policy, App Store listing, app feedback form, or developer website?
   **Answer:** Include a link to the existing privacy policy only.
20. What app version and "last updated" date should appear on the support page, and how should users learn about future changes?
   **Answer:** Skipped.
