Timer One

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Timer One does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. The timer runs locally on your device, with no account required.

This policy explains how Timer One ("the App") handles information when you use it. The App lets you choose a duration, start a countdown, and hear a tone when the countdown reaches zero.

Information collection and use

We do not collect personal information or usage data through the App. The App does not require registration or ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal details. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

On-device operation and storage

Your selected duration and countdown state are processed locally on your device, solely to operate the timer. The App does not send this information to us or to any external server, and an internet connection is not needed to use the timer.

We do not retain any personal data from the App, so there is no personal data held by us to access or delete. Deleting the App removes any locally stored app settings and data from the device.

Advertising, analytics, and sharing

The App does not contain advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell, rent, or share personal data collected through the App, because no such data is collected.

Apple services

Apple may process information through the App Store or iOS, independently of the App, according to your device settings and Apple's Privacy Policy. This policy covers the App's own data practices.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App or its privacy practices. Any updates will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the App Support link on the App's App Store product page.
